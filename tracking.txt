# 🛣️ Product Roadmap – The Simplest Bookkeeper

A lightweight, voice-first bookkeeping app designed for informal African businesses. This roadmap tracks the core features by development cycle (iteration) and product version.

---

## ✅ Guiding Principles
- Voice-first UX with low friction
- Designed for low-literacy, mobile-first users
- Offline-first with cloud sync
- Iterative delivery with real-world feedback

---

## 🌀 Iteration 1 – Working prototype(v1.0)
📅 Estimated: Week 1  
🎯 Goal: Capture and view basic transactions via voice

### Features:
- [*] FR1: Simple onboarding wizard (3 questions)
- [*] FR3: Accept and transcribe voice notes
- [*] FR4: Parse transaction data (amount, type, category)
- [*] FR12: Display chat history in bubbles
- [*] FR9: Generate daily income/expense snapshot
